MathVolt Support

We're here to help you get the most out of MathVolt

How MathVolt Works

MathVolt is a math learning app that helps users practice mathematics through fun exercises. The app features:

  • Multiple age groups (5-8 years, 9-13 years, 14+ years)
  • Different mathematical operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division)
  • Various exercise modes (4 Options, Type Answer, True/False)
  • Progressive difficulty levels
  • Score tracking and level progression
  • Multi-language support

Common Issues

App Not Starting

If MathVolt is not starting or crashes immediately, try:

  • Restarting your device
  • Updating to the latest version of MathVolt
  • Ensuring your device meets the minimum requirements (Android: varies by device, iOS: iOS 13.0 or later)
  • Clearing the app cache (Android) or reinstalling the app

Sound Not Working

If you're not hearing sound effects:

  • Check that sound is enabled in the app settings
  • Ensure your device volume is turned up
  • Check that your device is not muted

Progress Not Saving

If your game progress is not being saved:

  • Ensure you have sufficient storage space on your device
  • Check that you're not clearing the app data regularly
  • Try restarting the app after completing a level

Language Issues

If you're experiencing problems with language selection:

  • Check the language setting in the app's Settings screen
  • Try selecting a different language and then switching back
  • Restart the app after changing the language

Purchase Issues

If you're having trouble purchasing Premium:

  • Ensure you have a valid payment method set up in your app store account
  • Check that you have a stable internet connection during purchase
  • Verify that you're using the correct app store account
  • Contact Apple App Store or Google Play Store support for payment processing issues

Restore Purchases

If you previously purchased Premium but it's not showing as active:

  • Open MathVolt and go to the Premium screen
  • Tap the "Restore Purchase" button
  • The app will verify your purchase with the app store
  • Your Premium status should be restored automatically

Note: Restore Purchases requires you to be signed in to the same app store account (Apple ID or Google account) that you used when you originally purchased Premium.

Age Group Selection

MathVolt offers three age groups to customize the difficulty:

  • Young Explorer (5-8 years): Simpler problems appropriate for younger children
  • Math Challenger (9-13 years): Intermediate difficulty for school-age children
  • Math Master (14+ years): Advanced problems for teenagers and adults

You can change your age group selection in the app's Settings screen.

Exercise Modes

MathVolt offers different exercise modes to suit various learning styles:

  • 4 Options: Choose the correct answer from 4 multiple choice options
  • Type Answer: Type in the answer using the on-screen keyboard
  • True/False: Determine whether a mathematical statement is true or false
