MathVolt Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

Introduction

MathVolt is a math learning application designed to help users practice mathematics through fun exercises. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle data. This privacy policy explains what information MathVolt collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

Information We Collect

MathVolt does NOT collect any personal information. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, or any other personally identifiable information.

Information Stored Locally

MathVolt stores certain data locally on your device using platform-specific storage (SharedPreferences on Android, UserDefaults on iOS). This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to external servers. The following information is stored locally:

  • Game Progress: Scores, level completion status, and unlocked levels
  • User Preferences: Sound settings, language selection, and age group preferences
  • Premium Status: Whether you have purchased the Premium version (stored as is_premium)
  • Question Count: Number of free questions answered (for free tier limit tracking)

How We Use Information

The locally stored data is used solely to:

  • Save your game progress and settings
  • Track your scores and achievements
  • Remember your preferences (sound, language, age group)
  • Manage Premium purchase status
  • Enforce free tier limits for non-premium users

Data Sharing

MathVolt does NOT share any user data with third parties. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers, analytics companies, or any other third parties.

Analytics and Tracking

MathVolt does NOT use:

  • Firebase Analytics
  • Google Analytics
  • Facebook SDK
  • Sentry or other crash reporting services
  • Any other analytics or tracking services

We do NOT collect usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostic information.

Advertising

MathVolt does NOT display advertisements. The app does not use AdMob or any other advertising networks.

In-App Purchases

MathVolt offers a Premium one-time purchase (Product ID: com.math.mathvolt.premium). Payment processing is handled entirely by the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. MathVolt does NOT:

  • Collect credit card information
  • Store payment information
  • Collect billing addresses
  • Store transaction IDs, receipts, or purchase timestamps

Premium status is stored locally on your device as is_premium. All payment and billing information is handled securely by Apple and Google.

Internet and Network Access

MathVolt works offline and does NOT communicate with external servers or APIs during normal app use. The app does NOT use HTTP/HTTPS requests, WebViews, or sockets for its core functionality.

The only network communication occurs through Apple/Google platform services for In-App Purchase processing, which is handled entirely by the app stores.

Children's Privacy

MathVolt does NOT collect any personal information from children. The app does not collect personal information from children or other users.

Data Retention and Deletion

All data stored by MathVolt remains on your device. You can remove this data by:

  • Uninstalling the MathVolt app
  • Clearing the app's data through your device's settings (Android: Settings > Apps > MathVolt > Clear Data)

On iOS, deleting the MathVolt app removes its locally stored app data.

Account Requirements

MathVolt does NOT require an account, registration, or login. You can use all features of the app without creating an account or providing any personal information.

Permissions

MathVolt does NOT request access to sensitive device features such as:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Storage

The Android INTERNET permission exists only in debug/profile builds for development tooling (hot reload, debugging) and is NOT present in the release build.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
